The job titles of the two postings I reviewed were "Junior Data Scientist" and "Senior Data Analyst". Some of the required skills for the role of "Junior Data Scientist" include a bachelor's degree in Data Science
or another related discipline, and working knowledge of one or more scripting languages such as Python, as well as strong verbal and written communication skills. Some preferred skills include the ability to 
manage time and multitask efficiently, as well as current knowledge of the latest industry trends.  As for the role of the "Senior Data Analyst", some of the required skills include 5+ years in data analytics, 
BS in data science, statistics, or computer science; strong SQL and Python skills; and the ability to work independently and meet deadlines. Something that comes up on both is the need for "excellent written and 
verbal communication skills". I think this is important to the role of a Data Scientist because you are the one who is trying to understand and convey what is being shown through data. I wouldn't say anything in 
particular really stood out to me, but I did definitely notice how much of a factor your ability to understand Python is for landing a job with one of these companies as a data scientist/analyst.
